# 2026-11-22 — Open a node as its person in charge sees it

## Comment

> "when I click in, I want to see the roadmap of this node, the view the person
> who's in charge. so I can view things as other colleagues"

The owner's screenshot shows the top-level Canvas with "Onboarding & profiles"
(owner Priya Nair) circled.

## What was wrong

Clicking a step opened the node's own steps on the Canvas. For Onboarding &
profiles these were four unlinked items:

- They stood in one column at the far left, cut off by the dock.
- Nothing said whose part of the plan this was.
- Nothing showed what that person cares about first.

## Resolution

The owner chose "Node, as its owner sees it". The plan is in
[plans](../plans/2026-11-22-view-as-the-person-in-charge/2026-11-22-view-as-the-person-in-charge.md).

- **Banner:** an opened node shows whose view it is, with their first glance:
  Now with pressure, waits on, and blocking.
- **First view:** the page opens on Progress scoped to the node.
- **Their nodes:** the fourth toggle shows that person's own nodes.
- **Canvas:** a narrow Canvas level is centred.

## Status

The fix is in progress on `feat/view-as-in-charge`.
