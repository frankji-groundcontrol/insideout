# 2026-11-22 — The full roadmap of a node inside its window

## Comment

> "I want to see complete experiences like my own, not simplified to do"

The owner's screenshot shows their own Canvas page: the roadmap header, view
toggles and the flow of steps.

## What was wrong

A node's window showed a simplified card: status, owner, progress, evidence,
history. It did not show the roadmap experience the owner has on their own
page.

## Resolution

The window body becomes the node's full roadmap page, seen as its person in
charge, with every view and action, and laid out to the window's size
([plan](../plans/2026-11-22-full-roadmap-in-node-windows/2026-11-22-full-roadmap-in-node-windows.md)).
