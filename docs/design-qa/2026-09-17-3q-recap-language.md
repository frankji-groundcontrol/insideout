## North star

A recap issue, not a dashboard. One giant sentence, a strip of proof,
one orange slab. Evidence is not outcome.

Sampled from `REFERENCE-3Q.jpg` into OpenDesign `brand-spec-3q.md`.

Display: condensed grotesk, huge, tight. Body: Inter / Noto Sans SC.
No left rail. No Ink & Seal chops.

## Product mapping

Ideas, PRD, Commit, Roadmap, Evidence, Dashboard all use the same
chrome: sparse top, hero sentence, story-strip, optional orange slab.
