# 2026-11-21 — Roadmap canvas: the band fits its cards

A lone card no longer sits offset inside an over-tall band on the Canvas view.

- The band reserves the fold gutter only when it holds a branch, and has
  explicit padding instead of the dock height it used to inherit.
- The minimap and the full-width rule above it show only when there are at
  least two bands.

The owner's comment and the details are in
[the design QA record](../design-qa/2026-11-21-roadmap-canvas-band.md).
